# Board quiz: the game, the host page and the lobby

The Jeopardy-style board: phases, specials, the final round, the host's lobby and controls. Rules live in `lib/game.js` (pure reducer) and `lib/crazy.js`; the host page is `public/js/host.js`, the TV `public/js/display.js`, the phone `public/js/phone.js`. The hard rules (pure reducer, who sees what) are in CLAUDE.md.

## Final round

the TV shows the category and bet ticks, then the question with answer ticks, then judge cards that fill in as the host marks each team. The right answer appears when every team is judged. The host's final screen has Correct/Wrong per team (click again to clear). On phones, `stageKeyFor()` means the stage is only rebuilt when something that team sees changes, so typing a bet or answer isn't wiped out by other teams' updates. Teams with no points to bet send 0 automatically.

## Round 2

(`set.round2 = {categories}`, setting `round2`, on by default, lobby tick `#round2-setting` shown only when the quiz has one): `state.round` is 1 or 2, and `boardOf(s)` (crazy.js, re-exported by game.js) is the board being played; everything that reads the board goes through it. `hostView`/`displayView` put the current board in `set.categories` (the host also keeps `set.round2`), so the screens don't care which round it is, and views carry `round`. `endBoard()` in round 1 with a round 2 runs `startRound2()`: `round = 2`, fresh `used`, `picker = lastPlace()`, specials re-placed (`placeAllSpecials()`), pot kept, phase `round2` (the intro; event `round2`, phone status `round2`, music mood `board`), and `next` goes to `board`. Validation problems on round 2 have `r: 2`; the editor's `boardParts(r)` draws either board and `openQuestion(c, i, r)` edits it; AI-filled marks use `2:c-i`. `gapsOf()` gives round 2 tiles `r: 2` and `names2`; `buildRequest({round2, round2Topics})` and `parseReply(…, {round2})` handle making one.

## Picking on the phone

(setting `phonePick`, on by default): `phoneView` gives the picking team `canPick` and `board` (category names, values and `used` only, never questions, answers, files or specials) while the phase is `board`. The phone sends `pick {c, i}`, and the server adds its `teamId`. `pick` in game.js refuses non-integer tiles and, when a `teamId` is given, anyone but `state.picker` or the setting being off. Host clicks (no `teamId`) always work. The phone shows categories first, then their points (`pickStage()`, `pickCat`). The host's board hint and the TV say the team picks on its phone when that phone is connected.

## Single-screen mode

(no separate TV screen, `tvs === 0` in the state message): once the game starts, the host page shows the TV view in an iframe (`/display?embedded`, which says `embedded: true` in its hello and isn't counted in `tvs`) with the controls underneath. The embedded TV makes board tiles clickable and passes key presses and tile clicks to the host page with `postMessage` (`handleKey()` in host.js). The freeze choice and final judging get team buttons in the controls bar. H (`peek`) switches to the normal host view with a warning note; F is full screen. The iframe is kept between updates (moving it would reload it) and removed as soon as a real TV screen connects. Sounds and music play in the iframe (`displays` counts it), and it unlocks audio itself.

## Kaosmodus / Crazy mode

(`lib/crazy.js`, setting `crazy`: off/some/lots, plus `crazyExclude`: the kinds the host unticked in the lobby): at `start` the server rolls a `seed`, and `placeSpecials()` fills `state.specials` (`{"c-i": kind}`, about 1 in 8 or 1 in 4 tiles, a triple and a bomb first unless excluded, at most one turbo, jackpot, rescue and double, and fewer if too few kinds are left; each goes in the category with the fewest specials so far, and the one-team kinds in `SOLO` (hotseat, rescue, turbo, double) never go on a question with a picture, nor does turbo pull one into its queue). Picking a special tile goes to phase `special` (the TV reveal, `event: special`). `next` runs `startSpecial()`: triple ×3 (`q.baseValue`), bomb (instant loss of the value, result `boom`, no question), hotseat/rescue/turbo set `q.solo` (only that team answers: `arm` goes straight to `answering`, no buzzing, no reopen, and rescue and turbo have no penalty), jackpot adds `state.pot` (all points lost while specials exist) on a right answer, freeze puts `q.frozen` (chosen on the picking phone with `freezePick {target}`, status `freezeChoose`, or with the host action `freeze`) in `lockedOut`, and double (Daily Double) takes `q.bet` during `special` (`bet {amount}` from the picking phone, status `bet`, `view.betting = {bet, max, value}`, or from the host; 0 to `maxBet()` = the score or the board's top value), then sets `q.value` to the bet (the tile's value if none), `q.solo` and `q.fullPenalty` (a wrong answer costs the whole bet whatever `penalty` says).

## Specials placed by hand

a question can have `special` (one of `KINDS`, validated as `bad-special`, kept by `clean()` in store.js, never on the final). `ownSpecials(set)` collects them; at `start` they play when the setting `quizSpecials` is on (default, lobby tick shown only when the quiz has some), even with Kaosmodus off, and `placeSpecials(s, level, exclude, own)` adds random ones on other tiles, counting the own ones towards the number. `displayView` rebuilds the questions without them. The editor's question dialog has a **Special tile** select and the tile shows the icon. Turbo keeps `state.turbo` (`{team, queue, n, total}`, 3 questions, 5 s each), and `next` after a reveal opens the next queued tile by itself.

**Secrets:** `displayView` deletes `specials`, `rng` and `pot`, and `phoneView` only gets `special`/`solo`/`frozen` of the open question. Tests place specials with `start {specials}` (the server strips that from host commands) or `hub.dispatch`. Icons and rule texts are in `public/js/specials.js`.

## Lobby settings panel

(`#settings` in host.html): a Quiz block (picker, language, round 2, final + its seconds, own specials, the quiz-languages tick; always open), then native `<details class="settings-group">` sections: `#group-crazy` (open by default), `#group-rules`, `#group-sound` with the nested `#music-files` last in it, and `#group-look` (**Look and branding**: the glass tick, the picture-credits tick `showCredits`, the new-version tick `updateCheck`, then the branding, see look-and-branding.md; the version line `#about-line` sits under the whole form), and `#group-remote` (**How players join**: the `joinMode` pills, then, over the internet, the status, **Try again**, the download, and the `#remote-pin-on` tick without a `name`). A Quick quiz shows `#group-quick` (status `#quick-status`) and hides the board-only parts. Order and defaults are the owner's choice (2026-10-10). Each `<summary>` has a status line filled by `renderSummaries()`. Language, penalty and Crazy level are radio pills (`.pills`): `renderSettings()` sets them through the form's RadioNodeList, and tests click `label:has(> input[name=…][value=…])` (`pickPill()` in e2e.mjs). Things that don't apply are hidden with the `hidden` attribute, not greyed out (final row without `set.final`, final seconds, below zero when the penalty is none, volume when music is off). Tests must open a closed section (click its `summary`) before touching what's inside.

## Main menu

(`#menu-btn` in the host bar, shown outside the lobby; host message `mainMenu`, `mainMenu()` in `createHub`): works like closing and restarting ProQuiz. The game becomes `resumable` (in memory; not saved right away, so the file keeps the game), `state` is a `newGame` with the same quiz and settings and no teams, and every phone gets `welcome {teamId: null}` with `askedTeamId` kept. `resume()` merges teams that joined in the lobby meanwhile: same id = the saved team (score kept); new ones (not a saved id or name) are added with score 0 and a free colour if theirs is taken (and `final.base` 0). `start` drops `resumable`. The end screen's "Back to the main menu" is different: it's `restart` (same teams, scores to zero).

## Undo

goes back exactly one step: every host step calls `remember()` (pick, arm, correct, wrong, reveal, timeout, next, end, freeze, setPicker, judgeFinal, adjust).

## Autosave

the server writes `autosave.json` to `PROQUIZ_DATA` (default `~/ProQuiz/`) about 250 ms after each change. On start, a saved game with teams is offered in the host lobby (`msg.resume`). The host sends `resume` or `discardSave`, and a new game keeps the saved settings. On resume, phones that already said hello with their team id get a fresh `welcome` with it. Tests always pass a temporary `dataDir`.

## Quit ProQuiz

(`#quit-btn`, a quiet button under the lobby's settings below `#about-line`, so during a game it sits behind Main menu → lobby and isn't hit by accident): `ask({danger: true})` with `dlgQuitTitle`/`confirmQuit`/`dlgQuitOk`, then the host message `quit`. `quit()` in `createHub` takes it only from a host connection (so laptop only, never the tunnel's listener, like `mainMenu`; a second one is ignored): `saveForExit()` (the waiting game or the lobby to the autosave, as before an update's restart), `{type: 'bye'}` to every client, then `onQuit` → in `startServer()` `close()` (300 ms for the goodbye and a pending autosave, flush, the tunnel and its listener stopped, sockets terminated, both listeners closed; `close()` is idempotent) and 300 ms later `startServer({onQuit})`, which is `process.exit(0)` only in the main program (`import.meta.main`) and a no-op in tests. Under an update's waiter (lib/selfupdate.js) code 0 makes `relaunch()` report `early: false` and `handleExit()` call `exit(0)`, so both end (covered in `test/selfupdate.test.js`). Pages: net.js sets `stopped` on `bye` (no retry on close, no reopen on visibility); the host's `stopped()` (stops music and clip, `view = null` so keys and timers do nothing) shows `#stopped` ("ProQuiz has stopped. You can close this tab. To play again, start ProQuiz again.", `stoppedTitle`/`stoppedText`); the TV and phones show theirs (`endedTitle`/`endedText`: "The game has ended. The host closed ProQuiz."). `.stopped` is a fixed full-page block in style.css. Tests: `test/server.test.js` (its own server with `onQuit`: refused from a phone, the TV and the tunnel's listener; from the host saved, `bye`, both ports closed, `onQuit(0)` once) and the end of `test/e2e.mjs` (hidden during a game, Cancel does nothing, OK; screenshots `host-quit.png`, `host-stopped.png`, `tv-stopped.png`, `phone-stopped.png`). The START HERE notes and the README name the button as the way to stop ProQuiz (closing the window still works).
