# Quick quiz

The multiple-choice mode (`kind: 'quick'`): phases, question kinds, scoring, reactions and the file format. Rules and views in `lib/quick.js`; the editor side is in editor.md and the AI side in ai.md.

## Quick quiz

(`set.kind === 'quick'`, `modeOf(set)` in validate.js; rules and views in `lib/quick.js`).

**Mode dispatch:** `newGame()` sets `state.mode` from the set (`loadSet` too), and `apply()` picks the handler with `handlerFor()` in game.js: in quick mode `quickHandlers` first, then the board handlers listed in `SHARED` (join, settings, loadSet, adjust, undo, restart, mediaToggle, mediaRestart); board-only actions do nothing in a quick game and `answer`/`skip` nothing on a board. `hostView`/`displayView`/`phoneView` hand off to `quickHostView`/`quickDisplayView`/`quickPhoneView` on their first line, so the server doesn't care which mode it is. game.js and quick.js import each other (only used inside functions). Phases: `lobby → section → ready → open → result → ranks → (ready | section | over)`, except that after the last question of the last section `result` goes straight to `over` (`isLastQuestion(s)` in quick.js; no top 5 before the podium, owner feedback 2026-10-11; the host's Space button then says `showPodium`, undo from the podium goes back to that result); Space is `next`, Esc is `skip`; `ready` ends by itself after `quickReadSeconds` (0 = wait for Space), `open` after the question's seconds or as soon as every player has answered. Each phone is a player (up to `MAX_PLAYERS` 100, colours may repeat, `full-players`; a board quiz refuses more than 8 with `too-many-teams`).

**More than 30 players is experimental:** it hasn't been tried in a real game (the Wi-Fi router is the likely limit, since every change is broadcast to every phone), so the host lobby shows `#many-note` (`manyPlayersNote`) over 30, and the README says so. With 100 the screens cope: the host's lobby list (`.team-list`, max-height + scroll) and side list scroll, the TV lobby shows the newest `LOBBY_PLATES` 48 (47 plus `lobbyMore` "+ n more") with `.horde` over 40, the section standings show 10 (+ `quickAndMore`), the podium 3 + 12. `answer {option, at}` from a phone gets `at`/`slack`/`lead` from `pressTime()` like a buzz, and `fairOn()` in server.js is `fairBuzz || mode === 'quick'`. `lead` is half the measured round trip (capped at `MAX_REMOTE_SLACK_MS / 2`): `pressTime` already corrects the way back (press → server) through the clock offset, but not the way out (the answers reaching the phone), so `answer` stores `max(openedAt, pressed − lead)` and a far-away player isn't scored lower. A press after `deadline` is refused, and the server's timer for `open` waits `answerGrace()` longer (half the slowest remote phone's rtt + 20 ms) so such answers still arrive. Points: `pointsFor()` (1000 at once down to 500 at the last moment, ×2 or 0 by `points`, plus the optional streak bonus `quickStreak`).

**Question kinds** (`type`, `typeOf(q)` and `QUESTION_TYPES` in validate.js; `state.q.type` and every view's `q.type` from `ready`): **`choice`** (the default, left out of the file; "Pick one" in the editor): a player picks one option, and a question may mark more than one option right, any of which scores (`isRight(q, option)`, through `rightSet()`).

**`multi`** ("Tick all that are right"): `correct` is a list (even of one; every option may be right), a player ticks options and presses **Submit answers** (`answer {options}`), and only exactly the right set scores.

**`order`** ("Put in order"): the options are written in the right order (3–4) and have no `correct`; `openQuestion()` shuffles them with `shuffledOrder(s, n)` (Fisher-Yates on `nextRandom(s)` from crazy.js, never the written order; the seed comes with undo and resume, so the shuffle stays) into `q.order` (`order[p]` = the written option shown at position p, **host only**, never on the TV or phones), `shownOptions(s)` gives every view the shuffled texts, `q.correct` is the shown positions in the written order, a player taps the shapes in order (each gets its number) and presses Submit (`answer {order}`), and only the whole order scores. `answerOf(q, n, msg)` checks the message's shape by kind, `isRightAnswer(q, a)` judges a stored answer (`{option|options|order, at}`), `rightOf(q)` is the `correct` list every view gets (sorted for choice/multi, in order for order); `isRightAnswer` drives `closeQuestion()` and `fastestOf()`. Scoring is all-or-nothing and the Submit press is the press time. `state.q.correct` and every view's `q.correct` (host always, TV and phones from `result` on) are a **list**, even for one right answer; `q.counts` is per shown option (choice: who picked it, multi: who ticked it, order: who put it in its right place), and `q.nRight` (from `result`) how many players got it right (the verdict lines `quickGotIt`/`quickGotAll`/`quickGotOrder`). Question texts never say how many answers are right; the TV's `.quick-style` pill and the host's badge say the kind. The phone's `q.mine` is its own stored answer without `at` (`{option}`, `{options}` or `{order}`), or null. Phone: `draft` keeps the ticks/order across redraws; `.choices.picking` tiles toggle `.ticked` with a `.pick-badge` (✓ or the number), `#submit-answers` (disabled until complete) and, for order, `#clear-picks` in `.answer-bar`; "The right order:" comes as numbered chips (`.right-chips.as-list`). TV: multi settles like choice (right/wrong tiles, bars of ticks); order settles with `.opt-rank` numbers on the shuffled tiles and `.quick-order-result` (the options in the right order, with how many placed each right). Host: `quickOptions()` lists the shown order (for order with the right place `1.` in front and `quickHostOrderNote`). Views carry standings entries `{id, name, color, score, rank, delta, move, places, streak}` (and at `ranks`, `ranks.before` from `standingsBefore()`: the top 5 as it stood when the answers opened, `{id, name, color, score, rank}`, without late joiners), `q.fastest` from `result`, and the phone's `behind`/`ahead` (the gap line). Settings `quickReadSeconds` (4), `quickStreak` (off), `quickPhoneText` (on), `quickReactions` (on) live in the lobby's `#group-quick`, shown only in quick mode (Crazy, rules, round 2, final and own specials are hidden). `resumeFix()` restarts a showing question on resume. Screens: `body[data-mode]` on all three pages; the TV's quick screens, the phone's shape buttons (`shapeSvg()`, `optionStyle()` in ui.js; `SHAPES` and `OPTION_COLORS` in quick.js), and the host's question view and sorted player list. Sounds and music moods follow the quick phases (`music.test.js`).

## Player reactions

(Quick quiz only, setting `quickReactions`, on by default, lobby tick in `#group-quick`): `REACTIONS` in quick.js is the fixed list (😂 🔥 😱 👏 😭 🎉; never free text). During `result` and `ranks` the phone's stage ends with `.reactions` (six `.reaction-btn`, 48px, `reactionRow()` in phone.js, hidden when the setting is off, which `quickPhoneView`'s `settings` carries); a tap sends `react {emoji}` (the **index**) and bounces the button. The server (`handle()`): only from a phone with a `teamId`, at most one per `REACT_EVERY_MS` 1000 per phone (extras silently dropped, `c.reactedAt`), then `reactionFor(state, teamId, emoji)` (quick.js, pure: null unless quick mode, phase result/ranks, setting on, a player in the game and a valid index) → `toRoom({type: 'reaction', emoji, name, color, avatar?})`, the same targets as `sound` (every TV in the room, the embedded one included, else the host; remote TVs too).

**Nothing is stored:** not in the state, the autosave or undo. The TV (`showReaction()` in display.js) adds `.reaction` (`.reaction-emoji` + `.plate.reaction-name` with the face, the name cut with … past 11vw) to the fixed `#reactions` overlay in one of six **lanes** (`LANES`, `laneFor()`: spots along the left and right edges, never over the bars, the verdict or the top 5; the lane quiet the longest is taken, the three lanes on a side start at different heights (`LANE_LIFT`, `--lift`), and a lane used twice within a second puts the second one above the others), `reaction-up` floats it up through the lower half of the screen and fades it over 3 s (`animationend` removes it; `prefers-reduced-motion` fades in place), `MAX_REACTIONS` 12 on screen (the oldest goes). Tests: `test/quick.test.js` (the rules), `test/server.test.js` (100 phones, the message, the rate limit, the setting), `test/e2e-quick.mjs` (`quick-phone-reactions`, `quick-tv-reactions`).

## Quick quiz file format

(checked by `checkQuick()` in validate.js, cleaned by `cleanQuick()` in store.js): `{v: 1, kind: 'quick', title, sections: [{name, seconds?, questions: [{question, type?, options: [2–4 texts], correct?, seconds?, points?, image?, audio?, audioStart?, unveil?, unveilStyle?}]}]}`. `type` is `multi` or `order`, or left out for `choice` (`bad-type` otherwise). For choice `correct` is the index of the right option, or a list of indices when several are right (any of them counts); `rightSet(q)` in validate.js reads both as a sorted list, so old files keep working. `clean()` saves one right answer as a number and several as a list (always a list for multi, none for order, and `type` only for multi/order, so older files come out unchanged), and left out until one is marked. At least one, all in range, no repeats (`bad-correct`), and for choice not every option (`all-correct`); an order question needs `MIN_ORDER_OPTIONS` 3 options (`order-options`) and keeps no marks; `seconds` 5–120 falls back to the section's, then 20; `points` is 1000 (left out), 2000 or 0. Limits: 10 sections, 30 questions each, options 80 letters. Problems use `c` for the section and `i` for the question (codes `no-sections`, `no-section-name`, `bad-options`, `bad-correct`, `bad-seconds`, `bad-points`, …). `clean()` drops empty options except the ones marked right (the marks follow their options). The samples are `quick-sample` (English) and `lynquiz` (Norwegian); each has one "tick all" question with two right answers and one "put in order" question (oldest to newest); no question text says how many are right.
